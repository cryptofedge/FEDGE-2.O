Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

FEDGE 2.O is published by Eclat Universe. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and who it shares data with. It describes the app as it actually behaves — if that changes, this document changes with it.

FEDGE 2.O has no accounts, no sign-up, and no advertising. We do not sell your data. The only information that leaves your device is the text you choose to send to the in-app FEDGE coach, along with a summary of your game progress so the coach can give relevant answers.

1. What we collect

Information you send to the FEDGE coach

When you open the FEDGE chat inside any game and send a message, we transmit to our own server:

  • the message you typed;
  • your in-game state — the display name you chose, your level, XP, coins, and how far you have progressed in each of the six games.

We send this so the coach can answer in context, for example by referring to the game you are playing or the progress you have made.

This is processed by an AI model. Your message and game state are passed to Google's Gemini API to generate the reply. Google processes this data under its own terms. Please do not send information you would not want processed by a third-party AI service.

Information stored only on your device

Your game progress — level, XP, coins, achievements, per-game completion, and any name you enter on a game leaderboard — is held on your device. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see it except in the summary described above when you message the coach.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • No account credentials — the app has no login.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or files.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No payment or financial account information.
  • No credit reports or credit bureau data. The app includes a credit-score game. It uses simulated data only. It is not connected to Equifax, Experian, TransUnion, or any lender, and it does not access your real credit file.

2. How we use it

Only to answer your message in the coach, and to make that reply relevant to where you are in the games. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. Who we share it with

One processor: Google (Gemini API), which generates the coach's replies. Nobody else. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. How long we keep it

Coach conversations are held in server memory to maintain the thread of a conversation and are not written to a long-term database. They are lost when the service restarts. On-device game data stays until you delete the app or clear its storage.

5. Security

Traffic between the app and our server uses HTTPS in our published builds. Model API keys are held on our server and are never included in the app you download.

6. Children

FEDGE 2.O is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

The coach is optional — if you never open the chat, nothing leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data. To ask what we hold about you or to request deletion, email cryptofedge@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may have rights under the GDPR or CCPA to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to processing; we honour these requests regardless of location.

8. Not financial advice

FEDGE 2.O is educational. The coach explains concepts; it is not a licensed financial, tax, or legal advisor, and nothing in the app is personalised investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Trading in the app is simulated and no real money is ever at risk.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and note the change in the app's release notes.
