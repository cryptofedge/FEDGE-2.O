WHAT IS FEDGE 2.O?

FEDGE 2.O is an AI agent ecosystem built to put generational wealth in the hands of people who were never taught how to build it. Credit, investing, trusts, ownership — the rules exist, they were just never handed to us. FEDGE translates them into something you can actually play.

It isn't a course and it isn't a finance app. It's a persistent AI brain running a suite of games, where every mechanic maps to a real financial or life skill. You learn the move by making it — with nothing on the line but XP.

01 // The Brain

A Persistent AI Agent

Not a chatbot that forgets you. Every session loads identity, curated long-term memory, and operator context from SOUL.md, MEMORY.md and USER.md, plus 54 domain skills spanning finance, crypto, music, legal and social. A 9-agent architecture delegates to eight specialists — one per game — running in parallel on a shared filesystem.

02 // The Games

Six Live, Playable Builds

Each game teaches exactly one thing and teaches it through consequence. Paper-trade a crash. Move a FICO score from 500 to 750. Sign a bad record deal and learn what termination rights are worth 35 years later. Built in Three.js and Phaser, running free on GitHub Pages — no install, no signup.

03 // The Layer

$ECLT on Solana

Eclatcrypto is the connective tissue — a token earned across every game in the ecosystem, with wallet connect, on-chain scores and staking. Crypto literacy taught the same way as everything else here: by owning something and finding out how it works. In development.
