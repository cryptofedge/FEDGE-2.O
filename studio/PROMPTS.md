# OpenMontage prompts for FEDGE 2.O

For videos bigger than the 30-second promos: open your `OpenMontage` folder in Claude Code and paste one of these. OpenMontage plans the video, shows you the script and cost, and waits for your OK before it generates anything. The brand block at the top of each prompt keeps every video looking like FEDGE.

**Brand block** (already included below):
> Brand: FEDGE 2.O by Eclat Universe. Dark background #03030A, orange #E87012, cyan #00FFF9, purple #A78BFA. Bold, confident, NYC energy, speaks to people who were never taught how money works. Always end on "Play free at fedge2o.com". Add "Educational game. Not financial advice." on the end card. Logo: ../FEDGE-2.O-push/docs/assets/logo.png. Character art: ../FEDGE-2.O-push/docs/assets/fedge-character.png.

### 1. FEDGE 2.O brand trailer (60s, 16:9)
```
Make a 60-second cinematic trailer for FEDGE 2.O, an AI-powered suite of 6 games that teach generational wealth: TradeStreet (spot pump-and-dumps), Credit Warrior (build credit from zero), World Stage (own your music), Trust Fund Tycoon (protect an inheritance), Generational Wealth (infinite banking), and Lock In (mental discipline). Hook: "They never taught us the rules. So we turned them into a game." Deep, confident narration, a hard-hitting instrumental, fast cuts, one beat per game. 16:9 for YouTube and fedge2o.com.
Brand: FEDGE 2.O by Eclat Universe. Dark background #03030A, orange #E87012, cyan #00FFF9, purple #A78BFA. Bold, confident, NYC energy, speaks to people who were never taught how money works. Always end on "Play free at fedge2o.com". Add "Educational game. Not financial advice." on the end card. Logo: ../FEDGE-2.O-push/docs/assets/logo.png.
```

### 2. Money-move explainer (45s, vertical)
```
Make a 45-second vertical animated explainer: "Credit utilization in 45 seconds". Explain what it is, why keeping card balances low helps your score, and one simple habit to start today. Use stat cards and a simple bar chart. Punchy narration with word-level captions. End by pointing to Credit Warrior at fedge2o.com.
Brand: FEDGE 2.O by Eclat Universe. Dark background #03030A, orange #E87012, cyan #00FFF9. Bold, confident, NYC energy. Add "Educational game. Not financial advice." on the end card.
```
Swap the topic for any lesson: *what probate is and how a living trust avoids it*, *what a PRO pays you for*, *how a pump-and-dump works*, *what termination rights are*.

### 3. Real-footage documentary short (75s)
```
Make a 75-second documentary montage about how families build and lose wealth across generations. Use real footage only, reflective but hopeful tone, sparse narration, with music. Close on FEDGE 2.O's mission: putting generational wealth in the hands of people who were never taught how to build it. End card: fedge2o.com.
Brand: FEDGE 2.O by Eclat Universe. Dark background #03030A, orange #E87012. Add "Educational game. Not financial advice." on the end card.
```

### 4. Start from a video you like
```
Here's a Short I love: <paste YouTube/TikTok link>. Make something with the same pacing and hook style, but about <topic>, for FEDGE 2.O's <game> game. Vertical, 30-45 seconds.
Brand: FEDGE 2.O by Eclat Universe. Dark background #03030A, orange #E87012, cyan #00FFF9. End on "Play free at fedge2o.com".
```

**Cost:** OpenMontage tells you the price before it generates anything. It works with zero API keys (free voice, stock and archive footage). Adding `FAL_KEY`, `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` or `SUNO_API_KEY` to `OpenMontage\.env` unlocks AI video, premium voices and custom music.
